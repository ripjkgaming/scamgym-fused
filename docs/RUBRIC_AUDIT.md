# ScamGym rubric audit

This is a deliberately conservative assessment. A polished demo can demonstrate product quality, but it cannot substitute for pilot data or proven adoption.

| Category | Before | Current target | Evidence judges can see |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Originality | 7–8 | 9 | ScamGym combines adaptive scam role-play, multilingual speech, private redaction, a hidden-tactic call, and a post-call pressure map. The explainable training loop is more distinctive than a scam-information chatbot. |
| Presentation | 7–8 | 9–10 | One mobile-shaped journey, senior-readable controls, clear progress, natural caller audio, an animated call state, evidence-led scoring, and a memorable printable exit plan. The golden demo remains deterministic during an outage. |
| Usefulness | 7–8 | 8–9 | It addresses a significant Singapore problem and teaches a repeatable behaviour: pause, hang up, verify. Four languages and no account lower adoption barriers. A 9–10 needs evidence from a real pilot with seniors or community partners. |
| Technical complexity | 8 | 9–10 | ElevenLabs Scribe and streaming TTS, OpenRouter structured outputs, a typed scenario graph, deterministic local scoring, sensitive-data redaction, runtime validation, retry/timeout fallbacks, and an offline demo path work as one system. |

Projected score: **35–38 / 40**, depending on delivery and whether judges require measured real-world impact for full usefulness marks.

## What to emphasise

- This is rehearsal, not another warning poster. Learners practise the exact moment when social pressure makes good advice difficult to follow.
- AI creates variation, but it does not decide the score. The simulator model extracts evidence; transparent application code performs the arithmetic.
- Tactic labels remain hidden during the call and appear afterward. That turns an emotional experience into an understandable lesson.
- Sensitive-looking data is redacted before evaluation, API keys stay server-side, and no account or database is required.
- The app degrades safely: typed input replaces failed transcription, captions replace failed audio, and the golden demo can run without live APIs.

## Remaining proof needed after the hackathon

Run a small pilot with seniors and measure pre/post recognition, safe-call termination rate, completion rate, language preference, and confidence after one week. A partnership with a community centre, bank education team, or Singapore anti-scam organisation would turn “potential for adoption” into evidence.
